1. Contribution

What the report shows that was not known before. A made-up example: the results are pending, so both claims are expected claims.

1. Claims

  1. expected On the Gammex MMC phantom, with each prior's weight set so that the edge of the muscle insert has the same 10% to 90% width, TV gives lower noise than QGGMRF in the uniform background. Supported by: Results, figure 1.
  2. expected On the Lungman chest phantom, at the same weights, TV's noise is lower than QGGMRF's by a smaller percentage than on the Gammex phantom, and TV shows flat patches with sharp borders that QGGMRF does not. Supported by: Results, figures 2 and 3.

PLACEHOLDER: the amount by which TV's noise is lower on each phantom, as a percentage of QGGMRF's, goes into each claim when the Results have it. If a result does not support its claim, the claim is revised or dropped, and the Contribution, the Significance, and the Prior work are settled again.

2. Not claimed

  • That either prior is better for diagnosis. The report has no reader study.
  • That the results hold for other priors, other phantoms, or other doses.
  • That any component of the method is new (see Methods).
