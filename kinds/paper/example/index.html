Paper blueprint: QGGMRF prior versus TV prior (example)

An internal report for a lab that reconstructs CT images by model-based iterative reconstruction (MBIR). This made-up example shows a front page while the argument is under review and the results are pending. One blueprint per card. Start at the top and read down.

Not yet submitted. Not yet settled: the results are pending, so the claims are expected claims. Reviewer: none.

The argument

1. Contribution

for review

Two expected claims, each waiting for its result. At equal edge width, TV gives lower noise than QGGMRF on the Gammex MMC phantom. On the Lungman chest phantom the gap is smaller, and TV shows flat patches that QGGMRF does not.

2. Significance

drafting

A reader who chooses a prior for CT reconstruction learns whether TV's advantage on a phantom of constant regions holds on texture closer to patients, and so whether a test on such a phantom is enough to choose between the two priors.

3. Prior work

drafting

QGGMRF (Thibault, Sauer, Bouman, and Hsieh, 2007) and TV (Rudin, Osher, and Fatemi, 1992) are both established priors. Which published comparisons of the two are closest is an open question.

The support

4. Methods

for review

No component is new. Both priors run in the same MBIR reconstruction, with each prior's weight set so that the edge of the muscle insert has the same width.

drafting
