No component is new. The contribution is the measured comparison, so the Contribution blueprint states measured differences, not a method.

Both priors have the same form. For an image x, the prior sums a potential ρ of the difference between each pair of neighboring pixels s and r in the set of neighbor pairs C, with weights bsr:

h(x)=∑{s,r}∈Cbsrρ(xs−xr)

The two priors differ only in ρ. The scale σ sets each prior's weight; p, q, and T are the QGGMRF shape parameters, held at the lab's default values:

2. Data and experiments

Two scans each of the Gammex MMC phantom, whose inserts have known CT numbers, and of the Lungman chest phantom, whose lungs have a vessel tree closer to patient texture, all at one dose. The scores are the noise in uniform regions, from the difference of the two scans; the 10% to 90% edge width at the inserts; and the mean frequency of the noise power spectrum.

PLACEHOLDER: the runs are designed as a separate set of experiment blueprints, linked here once that set exists. This page names only what a reader needs to judge the results.

3. Fair comparison

Each prior's scale σ is chosen so that the edge width at the muscle insert is the same for both priors, on a third Gammex scan that is not scored. One scale per prior is used for both phantoms.
