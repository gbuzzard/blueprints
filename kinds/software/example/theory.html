2. Model and objective

The noisy image is the clean image plus white Gaussian noise: y=x+n, with n of standard deviation σ in every pixel. The denoiser returns the x that minimizes

f(x) =12σ2‖y−x‖2+λTV(x) TV(x) =∑pixels(Δhx)2+(Δvx)2+ε2

Δh and Δv are the differences to the right and downward neighbors; ε=10−3 keeps the square root smooth.

3. Iteration

The gradient is

∇f(x)=x−yσ2+λdiv(∇x|∇x|2+ε2)

The step size τ must be below σ2 to keep the first term stable.
